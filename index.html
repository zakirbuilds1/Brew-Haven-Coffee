About Us

More Than Just
a Coffee

At Brew Haven, we believe coffee is more than a drink. It’s an experience. Every cup we serve is a blend of passion, quality and perfection.

We source from small family farms in Colombia, Ethiopia and Guatemala, roast in small batches each week and dial in every espresso by hand before the doors open. Our baristas know regulars by their order, and the croissants come out of the oven at 7 am.

Customer Care

Questions,
Answered

Can’t find what you need? Call us on (212) 555-0148 and a barista will help.

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

We deliver within 4 miles of the café, usually in 25 to 35 minutes. Delivery is free on orders over $25; below that it’s a flat $3.99.

Can I order ahead and pick up?

Yes. Choose Pickup at checkout and your order will be waiting at the counter, usually within 15 minutes.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Apple Pay, Google Pay, all major credit and debit cards, and cash at the counter. Online payments are processed securely and we never store your card details.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Tell us within 2 hours and we’ll remake it or refund you in full, no questions asked.

How do loyalty points work?

You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend. Every 100 points gets you a free drink of your choice.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Every drink can be made with oat milk at no extra cost. Our banana walnut bread is also egg-free.
